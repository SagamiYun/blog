## Kit Kyo

**Full-stack engineer · DevOps · agent development · ML systems.**

I build and ship across the whole stack — from product front-end and back-end, through the DevOps that keeps it all running, to LLM-driven **agents** and the tooling that makes them reliable. Lately I'm putting real time into the layers underneath: **machine learning** and **infrastructure**, down to GPU numerics and optimizers.

### What I do

- **Full-stack engineering** — front-end, back-end, APIs, databases — owning a feature end to end.
- **DevOps** — CI/CD, containers, cloud, networking, observability; keeping systems healthy and shippable.
- **Agent development** — building LLM-driven agents: tool use, orchestration, memory, and the plumbing around them. A small fleet of coding agents now does most of the typing in my own research, and every number they report has to trace back to a ledger.
- **ML systems research** — how numerical details like optimizer constants change what a model learns. The current papers are on the [Paper](@/paper/_index.md) page.
- **Upstream** — when a framework gives me wrong or non-reproducible results, I file a minimal repro with the project (PyTorch, JAX) and send a small fix when I can.

### How I work

- Write down what "wrong" means before the run, then report what came back, including the parts that didn't work.
- Small, reproducible artifacts over big claims.
- Edge and on-prem where it fits: run what you can on hardware you own, and send the rest to the cloud.

### What I'm learning

- **Machine learning** — moving from *using* models to understanding and training them (MoE, world models, RL).
- **Infrastructure** — distributed systems, GPUs/compute, and the substrate ML actually runs on.
